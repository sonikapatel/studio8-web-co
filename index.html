How we work together

A simple, collaborative process from our first call to a brand and website you can use right away.

  1. Week 1

    Strategy call & discovery

    We start with a conversation about your practice: your goals, your customers, what sets you apart, and what isn’t working today. I also review your current website, Google listing and competitors, so we agree on a clear direction before any design begins.

  2. Weeks 2–3

    Visual direction & iterations

    I share a visual direction for your brand and website, covering logo, color, type and your key pages. Then we refine it together, round by round, until it feels right for you and your patients.

  3. Week 4

    Delivery

    You receive everything ready to adopt: your launched website, plus a brand style kit with logo files, color palette, typography and social media post templates, all delivered as assets you own.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a project take?

About four weeks from our strategy call to delivery: discovery in week one, visual direction and iterations in weeks two and three, and delivery in week four.

What do I receive at the end?

Your launched website, plus a brand style kit with logo files, color palette, typography and social media post templates, ready for you and your team to use.

Do I own my website and brand files?

Yes. Everything is delivered as assets you own, so you are never locked in.

How much of my time will it take?

A strategy call to start, then short feedback sessions while we refine the visual direction together. I handle the rest.

Can you refresh my current brand instead of starting over?

Yes. I can evolve your existing visual identity to bring in more customers, or create a new one from the ground up.
